User Guide & FAQ

How to Save Videos

Download videos from any platform in 3 easy steps:

1
Copy Share Link Open X (Twitter), TikTok, Instagram, or Reddit and copy the video URL.
2
Paste in VidDownloader Open VidDownloader — the app automatically extracts thumbnail & video details.
3

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove watermarks on TikTok?

VidDownloader automatically extracts clean original streams for TikTok videos without watermarks when available.

Why did a download fail?

Downloads may fail if the video is from a private account, deleted by the uploader, or behind age-restricted login blocks.

Where are saved files stored on mobile?

Tap "💾 Save" in your Media Library to download files directly to your device's Downloads folder or Camera Roll.
